03 About the studio

VelvetDigitalLabb is an independent digital studio creating thoughtful, ready-to-use tools for people who want to build their presence online with intention.

We design workbooks, planners and templates in Canva — the tool many small businesses and creators already use — so there is nothing new to learn and nothing extra to pay for.

Each product is built to take you from a blank page to clear decisions: what your website needs to say, how it should be organised and what comes next.

06 Questions

Good to know.

Where do I buy the products?

All purchases happen on our Etsy shop. Clicking a product on this site takes you straight to its Etsy listing, where checkout is handled securely by Etsy.

Do I need a paid Canva account?

The templates are designed to be edited with a free Canva account. You can also print or export your pages as PDF.

How do I receive my files?

They are a digital download: once your order is complete, the files are available from your Etsy purchases page. Nothing physical is shipped.

Which product should I start with?

If you are still deciding what your website should include and how it should be organised, start with the Website Blueprint Kit. If your structure is clear and you need help writing the words, the Website Content Planner is the next step.
